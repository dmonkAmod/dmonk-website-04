Our story

It started with a walk.

December 2015 · Bengaluru

It was early morning in December 2015. A friend visiting from Zurich and I were walking along Gulmohar Marg at the Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru, talking about our work. He was working on a research problem called “Cognitive Storage.” He explained it simply: as we walk, we see thousands of things, but later remember only what feels important or relevant. His question was: what if digital storage could work the same way? That question stayed with me, and I started noticing how we store information and interact with it.

Moving to Copenhagen

December 2020

Cognitive storage the idea was exciting. At the time, the problem was becoming clearer, but not yet urgent, and the compute wasn’t really there. I was running my first startup, Qrera, building industrial IoT systems for machine health monitoring. COVID brought Qrera to a stop, and in December 2020 I moved to Copenhagen. We revisited the idea, first through an image-sorting application. After talking to users, we realised image storing and sorting wasn’t the real pain. Working at another startup made the deeper problem obvious: finding the right information quickly is difficult, and the problem goes far beyond search and sorting. The real issue is the mismatch between the speed at which information is generated and the tools designed to handle it. With the advent of AI and readily available compute, we could finally start thinking about information through context, relationships and intent.

Decision to build

December 2023

By now, the problem had become increasingly clear through my own experiences and the work happening around me. After building an advanced machine for a Copenhagen based deep-tech startup, I decided to resume the effort to build a new-age storage system one that thinks, not just stores.I started writing down the problem, talking to people and building a team around the idea.

Where are we heading?

Our long-term vision is to build a system inspired by the way the human cognitive system works. The human brain is an extraordinarily efficient and dynamic information system, while our current way of Operating, Organizing and Optimzing the digital world needs a rehaul. dMonk is a work in progress toward building extremely efficient and intelligent digital system for the future. Thank you for your support, and we look forward to having you with us on this exciting journey.
