Privacy Policy for dMonk Website Visitors

Last updated: 10 August 2025

1. Welcome to dMonk.ai ApS

At dMonk.ai, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard data collected from visitors to our website, including through services like Google Analytics, inquiry forms, and subscription features.

2. Information We Collect

a. Website Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website. This service collects technical information such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device information
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Referring websites

This data helps us improve our website experience and understand visitor behavior in aggregate, not at an individual level.

b. Forms & Subscriptions

If you choose to fill out a contact form or subscribe to updates, we may collect:

  • Your Name
  • Email Address
  • Message content or interest areas

We use this information strictly for follow-up communication and to share relevant updates when opted in.

3. Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses cookies for analytics purposes. These include:

  • Session cookies (expire when you close your browser)
  • Persistent cookies (stay until deleted or expired)
  • First-party cookies (set by dMonk.ai)
  • Third-party cookies (may be set by services like Google Ads, if active)

4. Social Media Integrations

Our website may contain links or embedded features from third-party platforms such as Medium, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, and YouTube.

Please note:

  • These platforms may collect your data independently according to their own privacy policies.
  • We do not control how these third parties process your data.
  • We encourage you to review their privacy policies before interacting with such content.

At dMonk.ai, we treat any personal information we receive via social platforms with care and responsibility.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. Data collected through Google Analytics is shared only with Google for analytics reporting. It is not used by dMonk.ai for any marketing or profiling outside of aggregate-level website optimization.

6. Data Protection

We take appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

7. Your Rights

As a data subject, you have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data.
  • Request correction or deletion of inaccurate or outdated data.
  • Withdraw your consent for communications at any time.

You can contact us regarding your rights at: security@dmonk.ai

8. Complaints

If you are not satisfied with the way your data is being processed, you may file a complaint with Datatilsynet – The Danish Data Protection Agency.

Website: www.datatilsynet.dk

9. Contact

For questions about this policy or your data, please contact: security@dmonk.ai
